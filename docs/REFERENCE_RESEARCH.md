# WORLDSEED — Reference Research & Systemic Design Principles

This document analyzes core simulation and interaction design principles from leading artificial life, historical, and planetary simulators to inform WORLDSEED's architecture without copying proprietary code, assets, or trademarks.

---

## 1. WorldBox
- **What It Solves**: Immediate, frictionless god-game sandbox intervention, rapid world painting, accessible zoom levels, and inspectable civilization progression.
- **Principle to Adapt**: Dual-layer manipulation — allow the user to step back and watch autonomous deep-time evolution or directly intervene via divine/scientific interventions (meteor, plague, uplift) with immediate visual and historical feedback.
- **What NOT to Copy**: Fantasy tropes, closed-source art, sound effects, UI button layouts, exact power icons.
- **WORLDSEED Application**: The World Lab modal and direct map interventions with explicit causal graph node logging and historical anomaly markers.

---

## 2. Dwarf Fortress (Legends Mode & World Generation)
- **What It Solves**: Deep historical causality, generation of rich historical chronicles, myth formation from actual geological/ecological events, linguistic place-name substrate survival, and archaeological ruin stratigraphy.
- **Principle to Adapt**: Every historical entity (monarch, city, species, war, artifact, catastrophe) must be indexed in a bidirectional causal DAG so that the user can ask "WHY?" and backtrack to primordial causes. Real catastrophes must distort into cultural legends over centuries.
- **What NOT to Copy**: ASCII/DFHack tile formats, proprietary raws, UI complexity without progressive disclosure, specific lore entities.
- **WORLDSEED Application**: The recursive "WHY?" Causal Investigator, Chronicle of Eras, De-Mythologizer engine, and Toponymic Archaeological Substrates.

---

## 3. The Bibites & Species: Artificial Life, Real Evolution
- **What It Solves**: Continuous phenotypic gene-space mutation, Lotka-Volterra spatial population dynamics, inheritable anatomical traits (speed, size, sensory modalities, manipulation appendages), and adaptive radiation under ecological stress.
- **Principle to Adapt**: Genomes should directly dictate biological capabilities (trophic tier, thermal tolerance, locomotion, predation efficiency) and condition the anatomical possibilities of any emerging civilization.
- **What NOT to Copy**: Specific gene formats, proprietary physics engines, custom neural network formats.
- **WORLDSEED Application**: The Genetic Phenotype Renderer and Phylogenetic Tree of Life with inheritable anatomical traits (manipulation organs, locomotion, cognition index).

---

## 4. Eco & Thrive
- **What It Solves**: Trophic carrying capacities, environmental degradation feedback (deforestation, soil exhaustion, mine tailings), and chemosynthetic/hydrothermal autotrophy.
- **Principle to Adapt**: Environmental feedback loops must be bidirectional — terrain and climate shape species, but civilizations and megafauna actively reshape terrain (deforestation, canal digging, pollution scars, erosion).
- **What NOT to Copy**: 3D voxel terrain engine, survival game player avatars, specific tech trees.
- **WORLDSEED Application**: Soil fertility depletion, environmental damage scars, dynamic river channel routing, and hydrothermal vent rifts.

---

## 5. Universe Sandbox
- **What It Solves**: Celestial mechanics, axial tilt, solar insolation, seasonal thermal oscillations, and multiple orbital/planetary perspective projections.
- **Principle to Adapt**: Planetary topology and visual presentation must be decoupled. A single deterministic spherical or planar world state should be viewable via multiple optical lenses: Flat Atlas, Square Board, 3D Globe, Snow Globe Diorama, Relief Slab, and Orbital Spacecraft view.
- **What NOT to Copy**: Real-time N-body gravity solvers, NASA textures, proprietary UI shaders.
- **WORLDSEED Application**: The 6-Mode World View Presentation Engine and Milankovitch orbital eccentricity/axial precession climate calculations.

---

## 6. Songs of Syx
- **What It Solves**: Multi-species urban cohabitation, logistics networks, infrastructure tiers, dynastic succession, and trade dependency vulnerabilities.
- **Principle to Adapt**: Civilizations should not be monolithic — they should support multispecies cohabitation, dynamic trade roads between river settlements, and succession fragmentation.
- **What NOT to Copy**: Tile art, specific fantasy races, UI layouts.
- **WORLDSEED Application**: Settlement infrastructure progressions, multispecies confederacies, trade caravan particles, and dynastic rebellion mechanics.
